DejaVuSans.ttf / DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf

Bundled here to render Uzbek Latin (oʻ, gʻ), Uzbek/Russian Cyrillic, and
Latin text correctly in exported PDF quizzes (app/services/quiz_pdf_export.py) -
reportlab's built-in fonts only cover Latin-1 and cannot render Cyrillic
or the Uzbek apostrophe letters.

Bitstream Vera license, with additional public-domain glyph
